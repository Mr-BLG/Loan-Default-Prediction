In this project, I tackled one of the most industry-relevant machine learning problems: predicting loan defaults using a unique dataset. Financial loan services are crucial across various industries, from big banks to government loans. A primary goal for these services is minimizing payment defaults by accurately predicting which individuals are at risk of not repaying their loans. This allows companies to deploy effective interventions.

For this challenge, I worked on building a model to predict which individuals would default on their loan payments based on a dataset of borrowers from 2021. As a new data scientist at a financial institution, the goal was to build a machine learning model to assess the likelihood of loan defaults, helping the institution allocate resources to borrowers at high risk.
